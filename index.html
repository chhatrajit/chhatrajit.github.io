About

Dr. Thokchom Chhatrajit Singh is an academic researcher in mathematics. His work focuses primarily on fixed point theory and its extensions in generalized metric-type spaces, including cone metric spaces, partial metric spaces, dislocated metric spaces, and related nonlinear analytic frameworks.

This website presents academic information, research interests, publications, teaching activities, and contact details.

Research Interests

Fixed Point Theory

Study of existence and uniqueness of fixed points under contractive, weakly contractive, rational-type, and generalized contractive conditions.

Generalized Metric Spaces

Research on cone metric spaces, E-cone metric spaces, S-metric spaces, partial metric spaces, and dislocated metric-type structures.

Nonlinear Analysis

Applications of metric fixed point theory to nonlinear problems, operator equations, and generalized mappings.
